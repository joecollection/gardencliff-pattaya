    U-Tapao Airport Megaproject — ฿290 Billion
    Construction formally commenced April 3, 2026. Phase 1 targets 3–6 million annual passengers, scaling to 60 million. The airport — just 30km from Wong Amat — is Thailand's most consequential hospitality demand catalyst, transforming Pattaya from a drive-market into a direct international arrival hub.
  • 2
    Eastern Economic Corridor (EEC) Investment Zone
    Chonburi sits within the EEC priority zone. BOI incentives, streamlined foreign ownership structures, and infrastructure spending of ฿1.7 trillion are reshaping the demand profile from leisure-only to a mixed business/MICE/leisure market — expanding high-rate corporate demand.
  • 3
    India & Middle East Source Market Surge
    India was the #1 inbound nationality to Pattaya (1.6M arrivals, 2023), now accelerating post visa-free expansion to 93 countries. Indian HNW leisure travellers drive strong F&B and spa spend, fitting Garden Cliff's food & beverage-heavy asset profile.
  • 4
    Wong Amat Supply Scarcity
    The Wong Amat–Naklua beachfront corridor has zero new hotel supply planned at scale. Adjacent land values exceed ฿150 MB/Rai — reinforcing the land value floor on this asset. Any new-build entrant faces ฿3 billion+ replacement cost, anchoring Garden Cliff's pricing power.
  • 5
    Record Institutional Appetite for Thai Hotel Assets
    2024 Thailand hotel investment reached ฿22 billion — a record. Average transaction size reached ฿1.8 billion in 2025. Chonburi/Pattaya ranked among top 4 acquisition markets nationally. Institutional cap rate compression of 6–8% creates strong exit optionality at this entry price.
